The Eggshell Plaintiff Doctrine (Jury Instruction Standard)

Under established tort law, a defendant takes an injured plaintiff as found. Even if pre-existing degenerative changes existed in the spine or joints prior to the crash, the tortfeasor is 100% legally responsible for all acute traumatic aggravation, symptom onset, disability, and resulting medical treatment caused by the collision.
